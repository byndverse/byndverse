The New Site

The old site was a list of links. It did its job. But the Beyondverse was never just a list of links — it's a named universe with real geography, real lore, and a reason to exist beyond selling prints. The new site tries to reflect that.

Here's what's in it now, and where things are heading.

What's Here

The Gallery is the centrepiece. Fifteen pieces across six canonical biomes — The Luminous Wilds, The Drift, The Threshold, The Outer Gardens, The Mirror Moors, and The Quiet. Each biome has its own filter. Click a biome, see its world. Click a piece, it opens full screen. More art is being added regularly — the goal is for every biome to feel fully inhabited.

Free Wallpapers are available directly from the site. Six wallpapers now, formatted for phone screens. This has always been part of the Beyondverse philosophy — not everything needs a price tag. Some things are just for you.

Virtual Worlds documents the Sansar side of things. Three worlds are live: Renegades Wonderland, Pixie Punk Palace, and BeLux World. These are actual places you can walk through — the Beyondverse rendered in three dimensions. The page has previews and links to each world.

The Studio — which you're reading right now — is a flat-file blog. No algorithm, no platform dependency. Just posts, published when there's something worth saying.

What's Coming

The biomes are the main priority. The Luminous Wilds and The Drift both have room for more — expect new pieces over the coming weeks as the collection grows. The Mirror Moors is also developing its identity, and I want to give The Quiet the weight it deserves.

More wallpapers are coming. The current set is a starting point — the plan is to build out each biome with at least one dedicated wallpaper so people can carry a piece of their world with them.

And there's lore. The Beyondverse has a history that predates any of the art — places, events, the logic of how each biome came to be. Some of that will start appearing here in the Studio, not as documentation but as world dispatches. The kind of thing you'd find if you happened across a journal left behind in The Threshold.

"The Dream Is Real." — that's the only promise this site makes. Everything else is just showing you the evidence.

If you want to follow along, the best ways are still Instagram, the Substack, and just bookmarking this page.
