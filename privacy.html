Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Who we are

This website, byndverse.io, is operated by Jay Salton trading as BeLux Beyond, based in Queensland, Australia. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at studio@byndverse.io.

What data we collect

We collect only what you actively provide through the contact form: your name, email address, and message. This information is sent directly to us via Web3Forms and is used solely to respond to your enquiry. We do not store form submissions on our own servers.

How we use your data

Your contact details are used only to respond to your message. We do not add you to any mailing list, sell your data, or share it with third parties beyond the services described below.

Third-party services

This site uses a small number of third-party services:

  • Web3Forms — processes contact form submissions. See their privacy policy.
  • hCaptcha — used on the contact form to prevent spam. See their privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site (pages visited, time on site, general location). This data is anonymised and aggregated. See Google's privacy policy. You can opt out via Google's opt-out tool.
  • Google Fonts — loaded on desktop to render site typography. Google may log your IP address.
  • Substack, Instagram, YouTube, X, Threads, Sansar — links to external platforms. Visiting those links is subject to each platform's own privacy policy.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish visitors and measure site usage. hCaptcha and Google Fonts may also set their own cookies. We do not use any other tracking or advertising cookies.

Wallpaper downloads

Downloading wallpapers from this site does not require you to provide any personal information and no data is collected in the process.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have submitted to us. Contact us at studio@byndverse.io and we will respond promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
