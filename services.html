Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

How do I send you information about my business?

Use the contact form on our Contact page. Tell us your business name, what you do, who your customers are, and any topics or keywords you'd like to target.

How long does it take to receive an article?

Starter articles are typically delivered within 3–5 business days. Monthly plan articles are scheduled and delivered according to your agreed publishing calendar.

What format are the articles delivered in?

Articles are delivered as Word documents or Google Docs — ready to copy directly into your website CMS (WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, etc.).

Do I own the content once it's delivered?

Yes. Full copyright transfers to you upon delivery. The content is yours to publish, repurpose, or use however you like.

Can I cancel a monthly plan?

There are no lock-in contracts. Monthly plans can be paused or cancelled at any time — just let us know before your next billing cycle.
